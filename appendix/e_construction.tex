\subsection{Source corpus}
\label{app:corpus}

% src: src/reprocli_data/pipeline/index.py:1-4,18-24,49-75; src/reprocli_data/pipeline/sources.py:32-60,115; src/reprocli_data/pipeline/supplements.py:1-5,85-175,184-233; src/reprocli_data/pipeline/bundle.py:30-56,150-210; src/reprocli_data/build_dataset.py:119-160; notes/DATASET.md:114-146
The corpus starts from the NeurIPS 2025 index of the \emph{ai-conferences}
collection~\citep{neurips2025corpus}, which carries a pre-matched arXiv
identifier. A row is kept only when that identifier is well formed, so no
stage pairs a paper with an arXiv entry by title. Two fetch stages retrieve
each arXiv e-print package and each OpenReview supplement, matched by forum
identifier. The bundle stage writes one row per paper with the decoded
\texttt{.tex} text and the supplement files. The snapshot holds 3,414 bundled
papers, of which 3,410 carry at least one \texttt{.tex} file and 1,547 carry a
supplement.

\subsection{Stage I: artifact-verified classification}
\label{app:classifier}

% src: src/reprocli_vllm/config/config.py:7-13,16,28-32; src/reprocli_serve/profiles.py:65-74; src/reprocli_vllm/tools/web_tools.py:22-32 at commit 96d7f7f^; src/reprocli_vllm/runtime/loop_guards.py:1-66; src/reprocli_vllm/runtime/tool_loop.py:126-129,184-215; src/reprocli_vllm/runtime/run_health.py:17-27,29-46,57-70; src/reprocli_vllm/schema/output.py:8-20,92-154; prompts/prompt.txt:33-63,324-333; notes/DATASET.md:156-261
MiniMax-M2.7~\citep{minimax2026m2}, served on vLLM at a 196,608-token context
with requests capped at 128,000 input tokens, classified 1,000 papers sampled
uniformly at random from the LaTeX-backed corpus on 2026-06-11. Each paper
drives an agentic loop over ten tools, five over GitHub, three over Hugging
Face, a URL fetch, and a supplement reader, and every artifact signal records
how a tool established it. Three guards bound the loop, a 10-round cap, a
repeated-call cutoff, and a context budget, all surfaced in the verification
status. Only rows whose applicable signals were tool-established and whose loop
exited naturally enter the pool. A final tool-free turn returns a strict JSON
record carrying no score or tier, and the saved tool conversation makes every
call re-openable. Listing~\ref{lst:classifier-prompt} is that prompt in full, with
\texttt{\{PAPER\_TEXT\}} standing for the rendered paper, its supplement
manifest with text excerpts, and its \LaTeX{} source.

\subsection{Deterministic extraction}
\label{app:extraction}

% src: src/reprocli_vllm/schema/output.py:157-225; src/reprocli_vllm/audit/h100.py:1-95; prompts/prompt.txt:390-420,456-483; notes/DATASET.md:228-272
Score and tier are computed in code from the four signal booleans. Missing code
adds 2, missing weights adds 1, and missing data adds 3 unless the dataset is a
standard public benchmark. Score 0 is \texttt{Easy}, 1 is \texttt{Medium}, and 2
is \texttt{Hard}, the lockfile names for Run, Retrain, and Reimplement. Score 3
stays \texttt{Hard} when the data are available or standard and is otherwise
\texttt{Artifact-Blocked}, as is any higher score. The code recomputes GPU count
times wall-clock hours times the multiplier of
Table~\ref{tab:h100-multipliers}, flags a mismatch above 20\% of the larger
value, assigns a band from the ladder 0--8, 8--32, 32--96, and above 96
H100-hours, and raises a human-review flag when the basis is unspecified or the
recompute disagrees.

\subsection{Stage II: compute capping and stratified selection}
\label{app:selection}

% src: src/reprocli_vllm/audit/select_pool.py:39-134; notes/DATASET.md:289-336,360-372
Selection admits a row only when its verification status is verified, its tier
is an evaluation tier, and its audited compute is known and under the cap.
Audited compute takes the recomputed product when the arithmetic audit flagged a
mismatch and the multiplier is at or below 1.1, and the stated hours otherwise.
Of the 620 verified rows in those tiers, 38 sat above the ceiling of the
selection pass, leaving 582 entering selection. That ceiling exceeded the
benchmark's cap, so 17 pool rows sit above 96 H100-hours. The cap was set to 96
on 2026-07-09, replacing the three evaluation-split papers above it
(Table~\ref{tab:swaps}). The pool target is split equally across tiers by
largest remainder, apportioned across compute bands by fixed weights, and filled
cheapest first with the arXiv identifier breaking ties, most expensive band
first so a deficit cascades down inside the tier.
Table~\ref{tab:audit-pool} is the pool the rule produced.

\subsection{Stage III: human audit}
\label{app:human-audit}

% src: tools/verify_app/README.md:1-16,36-82; tools/rebuild_splits_from_app.py:110-165; src/reprocli_openai/recheck.py:1-12,30-45,89-95,118-125,247-266; notes/DATASET.md:337-359
A human opened the evidence behind every availability call in the pool,
recording a verdict on each of the four signals. The
reviewer never types a score. A review form recomputes the score from the
verdicts with the formula of Appendix~\ref{app:extraction}, and the audit does
not re-judge compute bands. A verdict overrides the model one signal at a time,
disagreement flips the boolean and anything else keeps it, and the
deterministic scorer then recomputes score and tier. Reimplement-tier rows,
whose tier rests on no code being located, were re-checked with GPT-5.5 under
web search, and on 2026-07-13
each claimed wall in the tier was put to one adversarial prober and two
independent refuters.

\subsection{From the audited pool to the frozen splits}
\label{app:splits}

% src: tools/rebuild_splits_from_app.py:1-22,44-93,166-205; notes/DATASET.md:360-372
The split builder applies the human verdicts to the model values the reviewers
saw, recomputes score and tier, drops the papers whose minimal experiment calls
a paid or closed model API, and runs the Stage II selection rule over what
remains. The development split is then taken from the cheapest eligible papers
each tier has left, so the two splits are disjoint by construction.
Appendix~\ref{app:dataset-schema} gives the split sizes.

\subsection{Compute re-estimation of the selected split}
\label{app:recompute}

% src: tasks/h100-recompute-eval100.md:1-12,25-60,199-236
After the split shipped, every evaluation row's H100-hour figure was re-derived
in a pass completed on 2026-07-01. The figure is the compute of the minimal
reproducible experiment, evaluation-only when the weights are released and
otherwise the smallest training run that shows the effect. Each row was
recomputed as GPU count times wall-clock hours times the multiplier of
Table~\ref{tab:h100-multipliers}, dividing a reported total GPU-hour figure by
the GPU count first. Ninety of the 100 rows moved, the audited total fell from
4,426 to 1,922 H100-hours, and the median row from 35 to 2.5 hours. Four rows
whose released weights are only the base model were kept as small training
runs.

\subsection{Post-ship substitutions}
\label{app:substitutions}

% src: notes/Reports/reprobench_substitution_log.md:14-24,140-160; tasks/h100-recompute-eval100.md:117-160
A paper leaves the evaluation split only when its central claim is
unreproducible in the sandbox by construction. A model failure never removes a
paper. The replacement is drawn as the next-cheapest audited pool row of the
same tier (Table~\ref{tab:swaps}).
